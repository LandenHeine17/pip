Showing

Screens

Tap one to show it. Ready-made screens you've changed can be put back; your own can be deleted.

Changing it by voice

  • "Pip, show the morning screen" · "save this as the study screen"
  • "Put the weather up" · "take the calendar off" · "put the shopping list up"
  • "Make the clock bigger" · "make your face small" · "colour the clock blue"
  • "Put a note up called Hall of Fame with …" · "show the conversation, without room talk"
  • "Undo that" · "what can go on the screen?"
If something won't fit, Pip offers a fix ("I can make the face smaller to make room. Okay?").
