"Pip, pull up a YouTube video explaining matrix multiplication" "Pip, pause the video""Pip, play" "Pip, fast forward a minute""Pip, go back" "Pip, go to 4:30""Pip, start it over" "Pip, louder" / "quieter""Pip, mute the video" "Pip, set the volume to 30""Pip, a different one" "Pip, take down the video"
Say his name with each (or just say it right after he's spoken): said like this, they're done at once, without asking Claude. "Stop the video" pauses it; "mute" keeps it playing, silent. While Pip is listening or talking the video plays quieter (Settings → youtube). The video covers the monitor with Pip's face small beside it; taking it down puts the screen back as it was. It's taken down by itself a minute after it ends, or after 30 minutes paused (a setting). Nothing is kept: Pip restarting takes the video down.
