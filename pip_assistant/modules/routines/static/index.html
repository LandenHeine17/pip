Things to say

"Pip, goodnight""Pip, set my wake-up alarm for 7:30 on weekdays" "Pip, good morning""Pip, I'm heading out""Pip, I'm back"
Said to Pip in a routine's own words, it runs at once without asking Claude; in other words ("night night, Pip") Claude works out which one you mean. The wake-up alarm is any alarm with wake up in its name: as it rings every lamp comes on (Wake-up), and once someone stops it Pip gives the Morning briefing. Snoozing it doesn't. "I'm heading out" also starts keeping any timer, alarm or reminder that rings with nobody answering, for "I'm back" to tell you once. The morning briefing only reads the events and to-dos everyone in the room may hear. "Run now" does the routine for real and shows what Pip would say, without saying it.
