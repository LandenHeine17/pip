Hall of Fame

The funny moments, word for word, saved when someone asks (D-147)
Say "Pip, save that to the hall of fame" right after something worth keeping: Pip keeps the lines that made it, exactly as he heard them, with who said each. He never saves one on his own. These aren't memories: Pip doesn't carry them into every answer, he looks them up when you ask, and knows a few titles so he can bring one up (starred ones first). Anyone can say "Pip, take that one out of the hall of fame".
