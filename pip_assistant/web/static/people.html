Teaching Pip your voice: once your name is on this list, just say "Pip, I'm your name" (or "Pip, learn my voice"). Pip says hi, beeps, and five short sentences appear at the top of the dashboard: read each one out loud after the beep. It takes about a minute. Check with "Pip, who am I?".
Voiceprints stay on the Pi. Pip hears everyone in the room; to stop him listening, use the Listening switch at the top (or clap twice, once a clap microphone is chosen in Settings). Visitors who say "Pip, I'm Sam" are remembered for that visit only; if they live here, press Add to household.
